GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked

How is pricing handled?

Every project is scoped and quoted individually — shot list, location, permits, and deliverables all factor in. Send the details below and we’ll get you a number within 24 hours.

Which islands do you cover?

We serve events and shoots across the Hawaiian Islands; inter-island travel is quoted per project. [confirm]

Do you handle permits and airspace?

Yes. We hold FAA Part 107, file LAANC authorizations for controlled airspace, and secure DLNR / county permits where required. Some locations and dates depend on approval and weather.

What’s the turnaround?

Typically a first edit within a few business days of the shoot, faster on request. [confirm]

Who owns the footage?

You receive a usage license for the agreed deliverables; full rights and source files are available — we’ll spell it out in the agreement.

Are you insured?

Yes — liability coverage on every flight, with certificates available for venues and productions that require them. [confirm figure]
