THE EXPERIENCE

What is a 360° photo booth?

Guests stand on a platform while a camera mounted to a rotating arm spins around them, capturing slow-motion video from every angle — head to toe. Unlike a traditional booth that snaps a still photo, a 360° booth creates immersive, share-ready video. Every clip looks like a moment out of a music video, and it’s on your guests’ phones seconds later.

PRO TIPS

How to get an amazing reel

01

Move slow. A hair flip or smooth spin beats a frantic dance — slow motion loves intention.

02

Grab props first. Confetti, sparklers, sunglasses, signs — pick them up before you step on.

03

Jump together. Count it out — mid-air moments look incredible slowed down.

04

Play to the lens. Hold eye contact and hit your pose as the camera comes around.

05

Sync to the beat. Time your move to the drop and lean on trending audio when you post.

06

Show the ‘fit. Themed outfits and bold colors pop — this is your close-up.
