What is a 360° photo booth?

Instead of a camera that snaps one photo, ours rides a motorized arm that circles the platform while you move. The result is a slow-motion video from every angle, head to toe, with music and your event's overlay already on it.

How to get a reel worth reposting

Advice from the people who've watched a few thousand takes.

Move slow. A hair flip or a smooth spin beats a frantic dance. Slow motion loves intention.
Props first. Confetti, sparklers, sunglasses, signs — pick them up before you step on.
Move together. Count it out. A synced spin or wave looks incredible slowed down.
Play to the lens. Hold eye contact and hit your pose as the camera comes around.
Catch the drop. Time your move to the beat, then post it with trending audio.
Show the fit. Bold colours and themed outfits pop. This is your close-up.
